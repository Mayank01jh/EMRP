From first tap to ready bay

One emergency passes through four hands. EMRP keeps all of them looking at the same facts.

  1. You call

    Hold SOS, or speak to the AI hotline. Your GPS and phone number go with the call.

  2. AI listens

    Gemini reads the severity and symptoms and opens an incident for the dispatcher.

  3. Dispatch sends

    The dispatcher assigns an ambulance, or auto-dispatch picks the closest free unit.

  4. ER prepares

    The hospital sees vitals and arrival time, and gets the right team in place.

One emergency, three screens

Each person sees only what they need to act.

Callers

A single SOS button, a voice hotline in English and Hindi, and a live countdown that shows when help arrives.

Dispatchers

A live queue sorted by urgency, a map with every ambulance, route times for both legs of the trip, and one-click assignment.

Hospitals

Inbound alerts with paramedic vitals, toggles for beds, scanner and cath lab, and one-click acknowledgment back to dispatch.

Built for the day something breaks

Emergency tools cannot stop working. These safeguards are part of the platform.

No double dispatch

An ambulance is assigned atomically, so two dispatchers can never send the same unit.

No duplicate alerts

Pressing SOS twice returns the original incident instead of creating a second one.

Works without AI or maps

If the AI or routing service times out, rule-based triage and straight-line distances take over.

A record of every step

Each status change is written to a tamper-evident audit log.
